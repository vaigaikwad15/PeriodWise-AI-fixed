Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how Lunavelle is intended to handle personal information and menstrual wellness records when you use the application.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Lunavelle is a menstrual cycle and wellness tracking application. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information the application may process, how that information may be used, and the choices available to users.

The actual data practices depend on the application's implemented backend, database, hosting environment and connected services.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on the features you use, the application may process:

  • Account information: information such as a username, email address or login details.
  • Menstrual cycle records: period start dates, end dates and related cycle information.
  • Symptom records: symptoms, mood notes and other information you choose to record.
  • Profile information: details you choose to provide through your profile.
  • Technical information: limited information generated by your browser or hosting environment, depending on the application's configuration.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to support application features such as:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Recording and displaying menstrual cycle history.
  • Displaying symptom records entered by users.
  • Providing general wellness information.
  • Showing summaries or charts based on available records.
  • Maintaining application security and troubleshooting errors.
Important: These are intended uses. The application only performs the functions that have actually been implemented in its code.

4. Menstrual and Wellness Data

Menstrual cycle and symptom information can be sensitive personal information. Please enter only information you are comfortable recording in the application.

Access controls, secure storage, appropriate permissions and protected connections should be configured before using real personal health information.

5. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in a database or other storage system configured by the application. The exact storage method depends on the project's implementation.

Reasonable technical safeguards should be used to help protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Do not share your password with others.
  • Keep application dependencies and security settings updated.
  • Do not upload real personal information to a public demo without appropriate security and consent.

6. Cookies and Login Sessions

The application may use browser cookies or session mechanisms to support login and maintain user sessions.

The actual cookies used depend on the Flask configuration and any additional services integrated into the project.

7. Sharing of Information

Lunavelle should not share personal information with third parties unless the sharing is necessary for a disclosed service, authorised by the user, required by law or otherwise permitted under applicable law.

Whether information is shared with hosting providers, analytics services or other third parties depends on the services actually connected to the application.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain stored for as long as needed to provide the application's features or meet applicable requirements. The exact retention period depends on the implemented storage and deletion procedures.

If you want information deleted, use an available account or deletion feature, if implemented, or contact the application administrator. Do not assume that uninstalling the application automatically deletes server-side records.

9. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the application's capabilities, you may be able to:

  • Review information you have entered.
  • Correct inaccurate account or tracking information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled.

These options depend on the features available in the application and the laws that apply to your situation.

10. Children's Privacy

The application should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements and privacy laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the application administrator to discuss the available options.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Medical Disclaimer

Lunavelle provides menstrual tracking and general wellness information. It is not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional, medical examination or diagnosis.

Calendar predictions and cycle estimates are not guaranteed to be accurate and should not be relied upon as contraception or as confirmation of pregnancy or ovulation.

💌 Questions About Privacy?

If you have a question about this policy, contact the person or organisation responsible for managing your Lunavelle application.
