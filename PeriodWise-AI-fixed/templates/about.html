Your wellbeing matters

Lunavelle AI is intended for personal tracking and educational wellness information. Keep your account credentials private and use the application on a trusted device. The project does not replace professional medical advice. Cycle estimates are not guaranteed and must not be used as contraception advice or as a medical diagnosis.
