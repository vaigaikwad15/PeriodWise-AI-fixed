💗 Your Wellness Space

Your well-being matters every day. Explore simple self-care practices for menstrual health, nutrition, movement, sleep and emotional well-being. Choose the habits that feel right for your body and daily routine.

🌷 Small habits, mindful living

✨ Explore Wellness

🥗

Healthy Nutrition

Balanced nutrition supports general health and can help you meet your body's needs throughout your cycle.

  • Include fruits and vegetables.
  • Choose whole grains and protein-rich foods.
  • Include iron-rich foods in your meals.
  • Eat regular, balanced meals when possible.
💧

Hydration

Drinking enough fluids throughout the day supports normal body functions.

  • Keep water nearby.
  • Drink regularly throughout the day.
  • Adjust your intake to activity and weather.
  • Choose drinks that suit your needs.
🚶‍♀️

Physical Activity

Gentle movement may support mood and general well-being. Choose activities that feel comfortable.

  • Try walking or gentle stretching.
  • Take breaks when needed.
  • Adjust activity to your energy level.
  • Stop if movement causes significant pain.
😴

Sleep & Rest

Consistent sleep and enough rest help support physical and emotional health.

  • Try to maintain a regular sleep schedule.
  • Create a calm bedtime routine.
  • Reduce screen use before sleeping if helpful.
  • Allow yourself to rest when tired.
🧘‍♀️

Stress Management

Relaxation practices may help you manage everyday stress and pay attention to your emotional well-being.

  • Try slow, comfortable breathing.
  • Listen to calming music.
  • Write down your thoughts.
  • Make time for activities you enjoy.
🌷

Menstrual Self-Care

Pay attention to your comfort during menstruation and choose self-care practices that work for you.

  • Keep track of your period dates.
  • Change menstrual products as directed.
  • Consider a warm compress for cramps.
  • Record symptoms and unusual changes.

🌞 A Simple Daily Self-Care Routine

🌅

Morning

Start your day with water, a balanced breakfast and a few minutes of gentle movement if comfortable.

☀️

Afternoon

Eat a balanced meal, take breaks and notice your energy level. Rest when you need to.

🌆

Evening

Make time for a relaxing activity and record any menstrual symptoms you want to remember.

🌙

Before Bed

Prepare for a restful night with a comfortable bedtime routine and a calm environment.

🌸 Listen to Your Body

Every person's menstrual experience is different. Notice changes in your symptoms, energy and mood without judging yourself. PeriodWise AI can help you keep records, but it cannot replace personalised medical advice.

⚕️ Health Disclaimer

This page provides general wellness information and is not a diagnosis or treatment plan. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about persistent or concerning symptoms. Seek urgent medical attention for severe pain, fainting, or very heavy bleeding accompanied by weakness or dizziness.
